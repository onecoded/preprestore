03 · Eternal Harvest & Anastasia Bio-Resonant Seed Planting Protocol (Vault Reference)

Cool, Dry, Dark Seed Vault Law & Eternal Harvest Species

A seed is a living embryo encased in a protective shell with its own nourishment. Keep all long-term seed vaults cool, dry (with silica desiccant packets), and dark inside air-tight glass or clay vessels.

  • Perennial & Self-Seeding High-Value Crops: Ground cherries (early-succession food forest staple), Walking Onions, Rocambole & Porcelain Garlic, Saffron Crocuses, Jerusalem Artichokes, Aronia Berries (top antioxidant density), Red Clover & Dandelion herbal tea guilds, and Sourwood/Linden bee forage.
  • Living Willow Architecture & Fencing: Plant willow and cottonwood cuttings before spring bud-break to weave living wicker perimeter fences and multi-story shade canopies.
  • 5-Gallon Balanced Aerated Compost Tea: 1.5 lbs balanced bacterial-to-fungal compost, 1.6 oz humic acids, 1 oz liquid kelp, and 1 oz unsulphured black-strap molasses brewed 24 hours to inoculate roots and foliage.
